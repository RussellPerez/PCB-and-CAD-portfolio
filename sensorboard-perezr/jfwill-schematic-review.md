## Function
* +3V3 and INT1 are attached to the same lines, which might be a problem if you connect something other than +3V3 as INT1 at the 2x3 Connector. 


## Style
* Although SDA/SCL nets are connected properly, they are not currently labeled. Not so much a function issue, but it might be helpful to keep track of connections/potentially reduce length of some of the wires. 
Sometimes splitting up the schematic into its different components can be beneficial in terms of checking over connections. I think it's impressive how you were able to almost plan out what your physical PCB layout might look like, so these little changes can make your design even more robust!
